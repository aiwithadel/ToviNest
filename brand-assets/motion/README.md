**Spec:** 1080×1920, SAR 1:1, DAR 9:16, H.264 High yuv420p, 30 fps, CRF 19, AAC 48 kHz stereo 192 kbps, `+faststart`.

**Layout rules (enforced by `node render.js --qa`):**
- Every text layer, pill, badge, CTA and mascot stays inside **x 40–900, y 320–1400**, the overlap of the IG Reels UI, the TikTok UI and the IG grid crop.
- Bands: hook 320–520 · visual 540–1240 · chips 1080–1340 · CTA 1258–1344 · brand footer 1352–1400.
- Text animates in whole words or whole elements (no mid-glyph wipes), and every text layer holds **≥ 1.5 s**.
- Pricing: $49.99 each, 2 for $89.99. CTA: "DM to order · Ships across the USA". Claims: "Soft cushioned landing", "Always supervise children during play."

**Audio:** a light, original music bed generated by `_src/music.py`. It's synthesized from code, so it's royalty-free, at about −19 LUFS with −6 dB peaks, leaving room for VO or a platform sound. To swap it, mux your own track over the video stream.

## Rebuild
```bash
cd _src
python3 gen_motion.py      # build/*.html
node render.js --qa        # safe-box + hold audit; contact frames in build/qa/<video>/
node render.js             # ../tovinest-*.mp4 + covers (about 2 min)
```
